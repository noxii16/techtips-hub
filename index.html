Quick PC Tip

Open Task Manager with Ctrl + Shift + Esc to check which apps are using your computer's resources. Only disable startup apps you recognize and do not need.

Stay Safe Online

Before entering a password or payment information, check the website address carefully. HTTPS alone does not prove that a website is trustworthy. Never share verification codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

Get Better Results from AI

Explain your goal, give useful context, and ask for the format you want. Avoid entering passwords or private personal information into AI tools.
